中国人给时间写的诗Poetry written about time

IP 二十四节气海报24 Solar Terms Posters

二十四节气是中国人观察太阳、感知物候的古老智慧。甘米米和呱噜噜用春分、夏至、秋分、冬至四个节点，把海南的一年——木棉、椰风、稻穗、暖冬——穿在身上。The 24 solar terms are an ancient wisdom of reading the sun and the seasons. Through Spring Equinox, Summer Solstice, Autumn Equinox and Winter Solstice, the duo wears a Hainan year — kapok, coconut breeze, harvest and warm winter.
