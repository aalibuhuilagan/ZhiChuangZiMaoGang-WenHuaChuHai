故事一 · 黎锦与甘工鸟传说Story 1 · Li Brocade & the Gan Gong Bird

每一根丝线，都是一句情话。传说里，黎家姑娘婀甘把银首饰捣成银翅膀化鸟飞去，青年猎手拜和也化鸟相随——两只鸟比翼齐飞，「甘工！甘工！」日夜不息。黎锦上最美的双鸟纹样，因此有了心跳。看完漫画，再用纹样生成器织一条属于你的黎锦。Every thread tells a love story. In the legend, the Li girl Egan pounded her silver ornaments into silver wings and became a bird; the hunter Baihe became one too and followed — two birds, wing to wing, crying "Gan-gong! Gan-gong!" day and night. That is how the most beautiful two-birds motif in Li brocade came alive. Read the comic, then weave your own pattern.
