七仙岭下，一对比翼鸟 At the Seven-Immortals Ridge, a Pair of Inseparable Birds

很久以前，在海南七仙岭（七指岭）下，黎家姑娘婀甘与青年猎手拜和相爱，却被恶霸峒主强行拆散。婀甘将银首饰捣成银翅膀化作神鸟，呼唤爱人；拜和也毅然化鸟相随——从此两只鸟比翼齐飞，昼夜啼鸣「甘工！甘工！」，人们便把这对比翼鸟称为「甘工鸟」。黎锦与雕塑里双鸟并飞的纹样，说的就是这份忠贞与自由。Long ago at the foot of Hainan's Qixian Ridge, the Li girl Egan and the young hunter Baihe fell in love — until a ruthless chieftain tore them apart. Egan pounded her silver ornaments into silver wings and became a sacred bird, calling for her beloved; Baihe turned into a bird himself and followed. Ever after, the two flew wing-to-wing, crying "Gan-gong! Gan-gong!" — and people named this pair of inseparable birds the Gan Gong Bird. In Li brocade and sculpture, the two-birds-in-flight motif still tells this tale of devotion and freedom.
