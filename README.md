## 作品简介 / About

原创 IP「甘米米（黎锦白鸟）× 呱噜噜（椰林蛙）」带你看海南：黎锦传说、文昌航天、南洋骑楼，三个故事以 26 格图文漫剧、AI 短视频、AI 音乐与多语配音呈现；全站中 / EN / 中英对照三键切换，含互动小游戏、IP 表情包、节气海报、落地蓝图与 IP 手办商店。

Original IPs "Ganmimi (Li brocade white bird) × Gualulu (coconut-grove frog)" take you through Hainan: Li Brocade legend, Wenchang spaceflight and Nanyang Qilou — told as a 26-panel comic, an AI film, AI music and multilingual voice. The whole site switches between 中文 / EN / bilingual, with a mini-game, stickers, solar-term posters, an offline blueprint and an IP figurine store.
